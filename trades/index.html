The work between the jobs is what costs you the jobs

A trade business doesn't lose money on the tools. It loses it in the gaps: the call that rings out while you're under a house, the quote you meant to send that night, the invoice that goes out three weeks late, the follow-up nobody owns. Every one of those is a small decision that has to happen at the right moment — and when it depends on memory, it simply doesn't happen.

Business automation in Newcastle isn't about replacing the way you work. It's about putting something behind the phone and behind the paperwork that catches what would otherwise be dropped, then showing you in one place what needs your attention today.

What we automate

Four connected systems, built around the tools you already use. Start with one, or start with the audit and find out which one is costing you the most.

AI receptionist for tradies

Every call answered, day or night. It takes the address, the job, the urgency and the best time, books a slot where it can, and scores the caller so you know who to ring back first. No call queue, no per-minute bill, no script.

Quoting automation for trade businesses

Your rates and your written rules become a starting price and a drafted reply. It won't discount, won't invent a number and won't quote a job that needs eyes on it. You read it, adjust it, press Send.

Questions tradies actually ask

How do I stop missing calls in my plumbing business?

Missed calls are a coverage problem, not a discipline problem — they arrive while you're under a house or driving. Put something in front of the phone that answers, captures the address, the job and the urgency, and either books a time or tells you who to ring back first. Our AI receptionist answers 24/7, logs and scores every caller, and puts the summary on your dashboard, so the only calls you make are the ones worth making.

AI receptionist vs answering service — what's the difference?

An answering service charges per call or per minute and reads a script, so you get a message rather than a booked job. An AI receptionist is a fixed monthly cost, answers instantly at any hour, and can quote from your own price list, book the job and write the record. You also get the transcript and a score for every call, which an answering service doesn't give you.

Can I automate quoting without losing control of price?

Yes — as long as it quotes from rules you set rather than guessing. We build the logic from your saved rates and your written rules: what you'll never do, what you always include, what needs a site visit. The system drafts the reply and the starting price; you press Send. Nothing goes out without you.

Do I have to replace my job scheduling software or CRM?

Usually not. The best CRM for a trade business is the one that already holds your jobs, and most scheduling tools can be connected rather than replaced. We build around your job scheduling, accounting, email and messaging, and add a dashboard on top. If a tool genuinely can't be connected, we say so in the audit instead of selling you a migration.

Does invoice automation work with Xero?

Yes. Raising the invoice from the job record, applying your terms, sending it and chasing the ones that go overdue is one of the most common builds. The job moves to Won, the invoice is issued from the same record, and nothing is typed twice.

What are real examples of business process automation for a trade business?

Answering an after-hours call and booking the callout. Drafting a quote from a website enquiry before you've finished the job you're on. Moving a job from Quoted to Won and raising the invoice in the same action. Chasing overdue invoices on a schedule. Texting a customer when you're on the way. Each one is small; together they're most of the admin day.
