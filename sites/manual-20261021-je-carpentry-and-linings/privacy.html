Privacy Policy

Last updated: 23 October 2026

What we collect

JE Carpentry & Linings collects the contact details you submit through the quote form on this site — typically your name, phone number, email address and a description of the work you need.

Why we collect it

Only to respond to your enquiry and to prepare a quote. We do not sell or rent your details to anyone.

Who sees it

Your enquiry goes to JE Carpentry & Linings and to the website service that delivers the form. It is not published anywhere.

How long we keep it

Enquiry details are kept only as long as they are useful for quoting and for the work itself, then removed.

Cookies and analytics

This site does not run advertising trackers. If basic, anonymous traffic measurement is used it does not identify you.

Your choices

You can ask us to correct or delete the details you sent us at any time by using the quote form on the home page and noting that you would like a change made.

Contact

For any question about this policy, use the quote form on the home page and mark it for attention.
