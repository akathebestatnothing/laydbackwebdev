How a pour runs

The order is fixed, because the surface depends on everything under it being right.

Plan and set out

Review drawings, confirm levels and establish grid lines before any pour.

Form and reinforce

Erect formwork to tolerance, place reinforcement and install embeds.

Place and screed

Continuous pour, vibrate and screed to the specified finish level.

Cure and hand over

Apply the curing regime, saw-cut control joints and hand the work over.
