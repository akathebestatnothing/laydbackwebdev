## Verified public evidence

The canonical Sensify Me page is Facebook page ID **61569873631692**, not an unrelated “Sensory & Me” business. Its introduction reads “Allowing and encouraging children to PLAY, LEARN and EXPLORE with excitement and confidence 🎨🌈” and lists Party Entertainment Service, Child Development and Children & Parenting.[1][9]

The full public page extract describes a “sensory science experiment party”, “bubbling potions”, “hands-on textures” and an invitation to “chat about our custom party catering packages”.[1][3] The associated photo album rendered 29 public attachments; local page HTML preserved the five visible attachment records from which full-size viewer URLs were independently resolved and saved.

The public reels tab supplies five named entries: a rainy first-birthday party for Eli; a racing-theme teaser; Spidey packs; a Christmas caption; and a book-your-next-event reel.[2] Each direct reel extraction confirmed its title and the Sensify Me byline. Racing, Christmas and the “Where it all began” cover are presented only as public inspiration, not fixed current packages.[4]

Logo inspection of the customer-supplied image verified **Sensify Me** and **Sensory Play for Little Explorers**, a pastel rainbow, script logotype and children’s handprints. Website font and colour choices extend that asset rather than reproduce another brand.

## Content boundaries

No verified business email, phone number, physical address, service area, price, availability, response-time promise, lead-time, pack size/contents, allergen policy, certification, testimonial, review score or age range was found. `Not yet rated (0 reviews)` was present; the site intentionally publishes no rating/testimonial rather than making a sales claim from this.[1]

Image interpretation is descriptive, not an expert chemical, ingredient, safety or suitability assessment. No “educational outcomes”, “non-toxic”, “food-safe”, “allergy-free”, “developmental therapy”, “childcare”, “award-winning”, “official superhero affiliation” or similar unsupported claims appear. Product photographs and cover captions are evidence of presentation and names only, not a complete contract.

The main browser tool could not start (Camofox unavailable), but independent `web_extract` plus local Google Chrome/Playwright succeeded for public rendering. Direct photo permalinks sometimes returned login/paywall errors. Public page HTML nevertheless contained media metadata used to match full-size URLs exactly. Full reel video/audio/transcripts and the album’s remaining 24 attachments were not retrieved. About/contact information beyond public category information was not visible. Follower/reel counts and dates are deliberately omitted from public marketing to avoid short-lived evidence.
