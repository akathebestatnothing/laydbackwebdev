# Marketing

## Target Market & Avatar
Parents and carers planning children’s birthdays or play-focused gatherings who want hands-on, themed activity rather than screen-led entertainment. This is the primary niche; no demographic, suburb, age range or qualifications are inferred.

**Positioning:** Sensify Me brings colourful sensory-play setups, party activities and themed packs into one enquiry path. The evidence supports custom party catering, sensory-science parties and Spidey packs; other themes are inspiration for discussion, not listed as packaged services.

**Primary conversion:** submit a party or pack enquiry. Facebook is the secondary route while verified direct contact details are unavailable.
