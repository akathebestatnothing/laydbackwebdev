# Positioning & Messaging

## Competitive Alternatives
Generic party hire, entertainer packages, online party-supply shops, or DIY sensory activities. These are observed alternatives, not a market-share or competitor-quality claim. Unrelated “Sensory & Me” brands must not be used as evidence.

## Unique Attributes → Value Themes
| Attribute | Value (“so what”) | Proof |
|---|---|---|
| Sensory-science activities | Parents can discuss a curiosity-led party with textures and potion-style play. | Facebook science-party post and public photos |
| Themes from real posts | Visitors can bring a concrete visual idea to the conversation. | Science, racing, Christmas and Spidey public reels/posts |
| Custom party catering | Package details can be tailored rather than pretending fixed inclusions. | Exact public post invitation |
| Spidey packs | A tangible themed-pack enquiry exists alongside party planning. | Spidey-pack reel and cover |
| Clear unknowns | Visitors know price, stock, travel area and ingredients need confirmation. | No authoritative proof found; explicitly labelled |

## Best-Fit Customer
Parents/carers who want to explore sensory-play activities for a children’s celebration or ask about a themed pack. Suitability by age, accessibility, allergy or venue must be confirmed with the business.

## Market Category
Party entertainment and sensory-play enquiry storefront, within Facebook’s public Party Entertainment Service / Child Development categories. No clinical, therapeutic, educational-outcome or childcare claims.

## One-Liner
Hands-on party ideas and playful packs for children who love to play, learn and explore.

## Brand Script (StoryBrand)
**Character:** a parent/carer organising an experience children can touch, make and explore.
**Problem:** generic lists make party fit, price, ingredients and availability hard to judge.
**Guide:** Sensify Me’s public activity photos, own wording and theme examples; unknown details stay visible.
**Plan:** choose party or packs → describe the occasion → ask for a tailored quote.
**Call to action:** send an enquiry or open the Facebook conversation.
**Failure:** avoid booking against an assumed package, price or location.
**Success:** a clear enquiry to confirm an experience suitable for the child and celebration.
