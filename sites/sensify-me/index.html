06 · Good to know

The useful details,
before the confetti.

Parties are personal. The right answer starts with your child, your celebration and a real conversation.

Ask your own question ↗
What does Sensify Me do?+

Public posts describe custom party catering—including a sensory-science party—and themed Spidey packs. Racing and Christmas-themed posts are also visible, but past themes are examples, not guaranteed packages. The current offer is best confirmed directly.

How much do parties or packs cost?+

There’s no verified public price to publish here. Share your needs to request a tailored quote. Asking is free of obligation in this website flow: there’s no checkout and no payment is collected.

Where do you travel, and what ages are suited?+

Service area, travel charges and age guidance aren’t specified in the evidence we could verify. Include your event location and children’s ages in the notes so Sensify Me can answer properly.

What about allergies, sensitivities or accessibility?+

No allergy-safe, food-safe, accessibility or therapeutic claim is made. Please discuss your child’s needs with Sensify Me, and ask for material/ingredient information and supervision details before booking.

What’s in a Spidey pack?+

The public reel names and previews Spidey packs, but doesn’t confirm a full contents list or size. Ask about exact contents, ingredients/materials, quantity, availability, fulfilment and price in your enquiry.

Does enquiring make a booking?+

No. Your enquiry is a conversation starter only. Timing, price, suitability, scope and a booking all need direct confirmation from Sensify Me.
