How we work

Set-out to Handover

01

Site Set-out & Chainage

Survey-grade set-out: chainage marks, string lines, level pegs, and string-line control for every pour.

02

Sub-base & Formwork

Compacted sub-grade, formwork to engineer's marks, reinforcement fixed and chaired to cover, hold-downs checked.

03

Concrete placed to grade, laser-screeded, finished to spec — broom, trowel, exposed aggregate, or coloured.

04

Cure, Joints & Handover

Wet cure, control joints cut to schedule, site cleaned, certifications issued, traffic management cleared.
