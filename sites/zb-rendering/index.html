What we do

Rendering & specialist finishes for Adelaide homes

Render & texture coating

Traditional and acrylic render systems. Float, sponge, bagged and trowel textures matched to existing or specified finish.

Venetian plaster

Multi-layer polished plaster with overlapping trowel arcs. Burnished to a deep, marble-like sheen that catches raking light.

Microcement

Seamless, thin-film cementitious coating for walls, floors and wet areas. Water-resistant, joint-free, contemporary minimal aesthetic.

White set finishing

Smooth gypsum-based set coat over brick, block or blueboard. Level 4-5 finish ready for paint - no texture, pure flat.

Feature walls

Polished or textured render. Norwood, Adelaide Hills, inner metro - interior and exterior.
