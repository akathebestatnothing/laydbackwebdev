How we work

Project flow — ground to slab

01

Set-out & certification

Laser-levelled grid to engineer's marks, penetration sleeves located, formwork inspected and certified before excavation begins.

02

Excavation & sub-base

Cut to design level, unsuitable material removed, approved fill placed and compacted in layers. Vapour barrier and sand bed where specified.

03

Reinforcement & formwork

Mesh and bar chaired and tied to cover specs. Formwork braced, joints sealed, step-downs and thickenings formed for edge beams and internal stiffening.

04

Pour, finish & cure

Concrete placed to grade, laser-screeded, finished to spec — broom, trowel or exposed aggregate. Seven-day wet cure, control joints cut, site cleaned, paperwork issued.
