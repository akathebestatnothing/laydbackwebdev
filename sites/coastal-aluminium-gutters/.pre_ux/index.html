Why aluminium

Built for the coast, not against it

Steel gutters rust from the inside out. Aluminium doesn’t - it’s the only material that matches a coastal roof’s lifespan.

Zero rust

Aluminium forms a protective oxide layer - no red rust, no staining on walls, no replacement in 5 years.

Lightweight strength

One-third the weight of steel but rigid enough for long spans without sag - less stress on fascia.

Colour that lasts

Powder-coated finishes rated for UV and salt - no painting, no fading, matches Colorbond and Zincalume.

Seamless by default

Every gutter run is one continuous piece - no joins to leak, no silicone to fail, no callbacks.

Fire resistant

Non-combustible material - meets BAL requirements for bushfire-prone areas across the Central Coast.

Recyclable

100% recyclable at end of life - aluminium keeps its value and stays out of landfill.
