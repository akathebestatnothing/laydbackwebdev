What we fit

Guttering, downpipes and roofing

Aluminium only, cut to length on site and fixed with marine-grade fasteners. Every run is checked with water before it is signed off.

01

Aluminium guttering

Seamless gutter cut to exact length, no rust and no leaking joins in the run. Colour-matched to your roof and existing fascia.

02

Downpipes

Aluminium downpipes with concealed brackets and pop rivets, sized to the roof catchment and plumbed to the stormwater connection.

03

Fascia and barge covers

Aluminium fascia and barge capping that keeps water off the timber, stops rot at the eave and gives a clean maintenance-free edge.

04

Leaf screens

Fine aluminium mesh over the gutter mouth. Leaves and debris shed off the roof, water still flows, and it fits new or existing gutters.

05

Re-guttering

Old rusted gutter pulled down and replaced in seamless aluminium, with downpipes and fascia rebuilt at the same time where they have gone.

06

Roofing and flashings

Aluminium sheeting and flashings for verandahs, carports and extensions. Lightweight, non-corrosive, and it will not rust through.

Material

Built for a salt coast

Steel gutter rusts from the inside out, and a coastal roof finds it fast. Aluminium keeps its section, its colour and its value.

Material
Non-rusting aluminium with a protective oxide skin, no red rust and no staining down the wall.
Finish
Powder coat rated for UV and salt, matched to Colorbond and Zincalume palettes.
Weight
A third the weight of steel and still rigid over the span, so the fascia carries less.
Joins
One continuous piece per run, so there is no silicone joint to fail and no callback.
Fire
Non-combustible aluminium, suited to BAL rated blocks around the coast.
