Privacy

This privacy notice explains how Bailey Metal Roofing ("we", "us", "our") handles personal information you provide when you use this website or contact us.

Information we collect

When you submit the quote request form we collect: name, phone number, email address, service type, message, and your consent. We do not collect any other personal data through this site.

How we use it

We use your information only to respond to your enquiry, provide a quote, and communicate about your roofing project. We do not sell, rent, or share your personal information with third parties for marketing purposes.

Storage and security

Form submissions are sent via a serverless function to our email inbox. We retain enquiry records for as long as necessary to fulfil the purpose collected, then securely delete them. We take reasonable technical and organisational measures to protect your data.

Your rights

You may request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal information at any time by contacting us at the phone number below. We will respond within the timeframes required by the Privacy Act 1988 (Cth).
