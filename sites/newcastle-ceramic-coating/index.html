Ceramic coating as a speciality

Newcastle Ceramic Coating describes itself as a ceramic coating specialist, coating vehicles, aircraft and boats. The workshop runs out of Medowie and covers Newcastle, Lake Macquarie and the wider Hunter.

A coating is only as good as the surface under it, so the prep wash, decontamination and inspection are part of every package, including the short 2-year coating for a car that is kept under cover.

Where the work is done

Medowie workshop, services across Newcastle and the Hunter.

What is the difference between the 2-year and 5-year coating?

The longer package uses a coating with a higher solids content and goes on a fully corrected surface, so it holds up longer on a car that is parked outside.

Do I need paint correction first?

It depends on the paint. The vehicle is inspected and washed before coating, and any correction is agreed before the coating goes on.

Do you coat boats and aircraft?

Yes, the profile covers vehicles, boats and aircraft. Send the details for a package price.
