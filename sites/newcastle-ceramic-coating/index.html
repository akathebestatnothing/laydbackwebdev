What gets coated.

Paint, glass, trim, leather and gelcoat. The prep and the masking change with the surface — the discipline does not.

Ask about your vehicle
01

Automotive ceramic coating

Multi-layer protection for paintwork, wheels, glass and trim. Hydrophobic, high-gloss, applied panel by panel with edges and trim masked.

02

Paint correction & prep

Machine polishing to remove swirls, oxidation and wash marring. A coating bonds to what is underneath it, so the defects come out first.

03

Aircraft coating

Exterior ceramic protection for airframes. Reduces contaminant build-up on leading surfaces and slows UV degradation of the paint.

04

Marine & boat coating

Salt-resistant ceramic layers for hulls, topsides and superstructure. Sheds water and cuts the maintenance cycle between washes.

05

Interior protection

Ceramic and hydrophobic treatments for leather, fabric, plastics and carpets. Stain resistance and UV defence where hands and sun land.

06

Maintenance programs

Inspection and top-up intervals that keep the layer working. Coating life is a maintenance story, not a one-off bottle.

Four stages. Nothing skipped.

A coating only performs as well as the surface beneath it. This order is the job.

01

Assess & measure

Paint thickness readings, defect mapping and surface inspection before a compound touches the panel.

02

Correct & refine

Multi-stage polishing matched to the substrate. Defects removed without chasing gloss over film thickness.

03

Decontaminate & prime

Iron fallout and tar removal, clay decontamination, IPA wipe-down. The panel is left clean and ready to bond.

04

Coat & cure

Layered ceramic application under controlled light, then an indoor cure before the vehicle is handed back.
