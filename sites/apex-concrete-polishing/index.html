How it runs

Four stages, in order

Each stage sets up the next one. Skip a step and the sheen will not hold, so we work to this sequence every time.

01

Look and quote

We inspect the slab, test a patch if needed, and quote the finish and area in writing.

02

Grind

Coarse diamond passes cut the floor flat and open the aggregate, with extraction on the tool.

03

Polish

Resin passes step down in grit until the agreed sheen is reached, checked as we go.

04

Seal and hand over

Sealer applied, edges detailed, site washed out, then care and re-coat advice.
