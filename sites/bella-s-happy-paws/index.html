What we do

Four ways we can help

Pick the one that fits your week. Every booking is matched to your pet's size, coat and temperament before anything is confirmed.

Daily walksOn lead, around the local parks

Your dog gets out at the same time each day, on their own or with a matched walking companion, and comes home settled.

Home visitsWhile you are out or away

Feeding, fresh water, litter and a proper bit of company in their own house, with a message to you afterwards.

Full groomBath, dry, brush and trim

Nails, ears and coat done at the table in a quiet room, in the shape that suits the breed and your routine at home.
