Our Process

Three stages. Zero shortcuts.

Washed

Low-pressure treatment removes moss, lichen, and grime without damaging tiles. Surface prepped for adhesion.

Repointed

Ridge caps re-bedded in fresh mortar. Cracked pointing replaced. Structural lines clean and watertight.

Sealed

Penetrating sealer locks out moisture. Optional colour coat rolls on in even bands - finished roof, warranted.
