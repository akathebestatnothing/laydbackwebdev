What we pour

Driveways, slabs and outdoor surfaces, set out and finished on site. If it is flat, load bearing and needs to drain, it is a conversation worth having.

01

Driveways and crossovers

Excavated, sub-base compacted and formed, then poured with a broom finish for grip and control joints cut where the slab wants to move.

02

House slabs and extensions

Set out to the plan, edged with clean arris lines, screeded flat and floated for the next trade to work off.

03

Shed and garage floors

Reinforced, level slabs for workshops, garages and farm sheds, finished hard-wearing where the traffic runs.

04

Paths, patios and outdoor areas

Walkways and entertaining areas poured to a fall, so water leaves the slab instead of sitting on it.

How the job runs

Four stages, in this order, every time. You know what happens on which day before anyone lifts a shovel.

Step 01

Site visit and measure

We walk the job, take levels and talk through finish, drainage and access. You get the scope in writing.

Step 02

Set out and form

Excavation, compaction and formwork set true to the line, staked and checked before the pour.

Step 03

Pour and screed

Concrete placed and screeded to level, then floated so the surface comes up uniform.

Step 04

Finish and cut

Broom finish for slip resistance, control joints cut to spec, forms stripped and the site left clean.
