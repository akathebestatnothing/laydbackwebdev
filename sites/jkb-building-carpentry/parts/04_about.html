About JKB

Joinery-Grade Craft, Building-Site Discipline

JKB Building & Carpentry runs on a simple idea: the precision of a joiner's bench applied to the scale of a building site. Every job — bathroom strip-out, deck frame, kitchen fit-off — gets the same layout lines, the same tolerance checks, the same pride in a tight joint.

We don't sub the critical path. The same hands that set out the bearers hang the doors. That continuity shows in the finish: square reveals, flush transitions, cabinetry that aligns to the wall, not the other way around.

Based in Newcastle, working across the Hunter and Central Coast. Licensed, insured, and on the tools.
