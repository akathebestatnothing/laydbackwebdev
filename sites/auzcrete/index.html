How we work

Slab process — start to finish

01

Site set-out & inspection

Laser-levelled grid, formwork to engineer's marks, penetration sleeves marked, inspection before pour.

02

Sub-base & reinforcement

Compacted road base or sand bed, vapour barrier, chairing and tieing of top/bottom mesh to cover specs.

03

Pour, screed & finish

Concrete placed to grade, laser-screeded, bull-floated, and finished to architectural tolerance — broom, trowel, or exposed aggregate as specified.

04

Cure, saw-cut & handover

Seven-day wet cure, control joints cut to engineer's schedule, site cleaned, paperwork issued.
