What We Do

Six core services - one team, proper gear, safe outcomes.

Residential Demolition

Complete house demolition, partial removal, and site preparation for rebuilds. Council coordination included.

Commercial Demolition

Office strips, warehouse teardowns, retail fit-out removal. Structural and non-structural works.

Asbestos Removal

Licensed Class A & B removal. Friable and non-friable. Air monitoring, clearance certs, lawful disposal.

Rubbish Removal

Construction waste, green waste, household junk. Skip bins supplied. Recycling-first disposal.

Land Clearing

Vegetation clearing, tree removal, stump grinding, site leveling. Prep for construction or subdivision.

Pavement & Floor Removal

Concrete, asphalt, tile, screed removal. Diamond sawing, jackhammering, surface prep for new finishes.

Strip-Out

Internal soft strip - partitions, ceilings, services, joinery. Clean shell handover for fit-out.

How We Work

Plan. Permit. Execute. Certify. No shortcuts, no surprises.

01

Site Assessment & Quote

Walk-through, scope confirmation, hazard ID (asbestos, services), fixed-price proposal.

02

Permits & Notifications

Council demo permits, WorkSafe asbestos notifications, utility disconnections, neighbour notices.

03

Controlled Demolition / Removal

Dust suppression, noise control, traffic management, progressive waste segregation, daily site logs.

04

Asbestos Clearance & Certs

Independent hygienist clearance, air monitoring reports, disposal dockets, site clearance certificate.

05

Site Handover

Level clean pad, compaction cert if required, all paperwork bundled, single point of contact.
