Scheduled servicing carried out to the manufacturer’s schedule, with the logbook stamped so the service history stays intact.

Air-conditioning regas

Leak testing & AC repairs

AC diagnostics & component repair

Compressors, condensers, blower fans, sensors and controls tested and repaired.

General mechanical repairs

Warning lights, noises, vibrations, cooling, steering and driveline faults investigated and fixed.

Brakes & suspension

Pads, rotors, shocks and bushes inspected, quoted and replaced before they become a bigger bill.

Air conditioning — the other half of the workshop

Cold air is not a side job here. The AC side gets the same diagnostic gear and the same test-first approach as the mechanical side: find out why it is not cold before anyone touches the gas.

Diagnostic scan

System scanned for fault codes, vent temperature and pressures measured, so the diagnosis is what the car is telling us — not a guess.

Regas

System evacuated and recharged to the charge weight on the underbonnet label, with the oil topped up as required.

Compressor & condenser

Compressor, condenser, TX valve and lines inspected, replaced where they have failed and pressure-tested after the repair.

Cabin filter & blower

Filter, blower fan and airflow checked and replaced — the reason a system can blow hard and still feel weak.

Leak test

UV dye and pressure testing to find where the refrigerant is going, because topping up a leaking system just pays for the same gas twice.

Controls & electrical

Sensors, relays, fans and climate control faults traced and repaired so the temperature you dial up is the temperature you get.

Questions about your vehicle

Servicing, air conditioning and what to expect when you book in.

Do you do logbook servicing?

Yes. Servicing follows the manufacturer’s schedule using the correct grade of oils and parts, and the logbook is stamped and signed so your service history stays complete.

My air conditioning is not cold. Is it just a regas?

Not usually. If the system is low on refrigerant, it has gone somewhere — so we pressure-test and leak-test first. Topping up a leaking system just means paying for the same gas again later.

Do you work on all makes and models?

Most makes and models, petrol and diesel. Tell us what you drive when you book and we will confirm straight away.

How long does an air-conditioning service take?

A straightforward regas after testing is typically a couple of hours. A leak or a component repair takes longer, and we will tell you what we found before going ahead.

Can I book a time rather than leave the car all day?

Yes — send through the rego, the model and what is wrong, and we will book you into a time slot and let you know roughly how long it will take.
