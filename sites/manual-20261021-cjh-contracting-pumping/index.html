What we do

Excavation and pumping for Sydney sites — residential, commercial and civil. One crew, one contact, plant on site.

Detailed Excavation

Trenching for services, footings, piers and pool digs, cut to line and depth with the right sized machine.

Dewatering

Pump Hire & Pump-Outs

Stormwater & Sewer

Shoring & Reinstatement

Trench shields and shoring for deep or unstable cuts, then reinstatement of driveways, paths and verges.

CJH Contracting & Pumping

CJH Contracting & Pumping is a Sydney excavation and pumping contractor. We dig the trench, keep the water out of it and get the job to a point where the next trade can start. Residential, commercial and civil work, big or small.

Excavation and pumping sit with the same crew, so a wet cut doesn't stall waiting on a second contractor. You deal with one person from quote to sign-off, and you get a straight answer about what the ground will do.

  • Excavation and pumping under one contractor
  • Machine and operator matched to the access and the depth
  • Pumps, hoses and dewatering gear on hand
  • Work sequenced around your program
