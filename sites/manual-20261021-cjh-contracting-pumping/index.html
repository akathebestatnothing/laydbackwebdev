Common questions

Questions we get asked

What areas do you work in?

Sydney metro and surrounds — the inner west, south, west and the Hills. Further out by arrangement.

Do you pump as well as excavate?

Yes. Excavation and concrete pumping are both part of the work, so the trench and the pour sit with one crew.

Can you get water off a site?

Yes. Standing water is pumped out and the site drained so the machine work and the pour can keep moving.

How do I get a price?

Send the plans, levels or a photo of the site through the quote form and we will price it. Message the Facebook page if that is easier.
