Privacy

01

What the form collects

The quote form on the home page asks for your name, a phone number or an email address, and a few lines about the job. That is all it collects.

02

Why it is collected

Only so the enquiry can be answered, and so the work can be arranged if it goes ahead.

03

Who handles it

Your details go to BTL. The form is delivered by the web team that hosts this site, so they handle it on the way through.

04

How long it is kept

For as long as it takes to answer the enquiry and, if the work goes ahead, to run the job.

05

Your say

Ring the number on this page at any time and ask for your details to be removed.

06

What is not done with it

Your details are not sold, and not passed on for marketing.

07

Links out

The Facebook link on this site opens a page run by BTL under Facebook's own privacy terms.
