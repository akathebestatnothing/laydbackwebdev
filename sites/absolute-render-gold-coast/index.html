Services

Render

Traditional and acrylic render systems for exterior and interior walls. Durable, weather-resistant finishes suited to Gold Coast conditions.

Venetian Plaster

Polished lime-based plaster with natural depth and sheen. Feature walls, bathrooms and high-end residential finishes.

Microcement

Seamless cementitious coating for floors, walls and wet areas. Thin-build, waterproof and contemporary aesthetic.

Repaints & Texture Finishes

Full repaint services with texture matching - stipple, trowel, sponge and custom finishes to blend with existing surfaces.
