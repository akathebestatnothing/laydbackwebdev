How we work.

Laser-guided grading

Dual-grade laser on every pad. Cut and fill tracked in real time - the surface reads level before we pack up.

Grade stakes & targets

Staked at 5m grid with laser receivers. Cut/fill called at each stake, spoil pushed to fill zones, final pass with the bucket.

Single-machine flow

One excavator does the cut, the push and the finish. No hand-offs, no double-handling, no arguments on level.

Spoil balance

Cut volume matches fill volume on the pad. Excess carted only when the design demands it - cost and carbon saved.
