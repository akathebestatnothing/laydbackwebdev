Tiling questions, answered

The things people ask before they book a tiler.

Do you waterproof the bathroom?

Wet-area tiling starts with waterproofing preparation. Membranes, falls to the waste and hob details are sorted before the first tile is laid, and we will not tile over a surface that has not been properly prepared.

How long does a bathroom take?

A typical bathroom is roughly 4 to 7 working days once the substrate is ready, depending on tile size, layout and how much prep is needed. Small-format and mosaic work takes longer than large-format tiles.

Can you tile over my existing tiles?

Sometimes. If the existing tiles are sound and firmly bonded and the finished floor height still works, tiling over them can be an option. Otherwise the old tiles come up and we start from the substrate.

Do you supply the tiles?

Either way. We are happy to lay tiles you have already chosen and paid for, or we can measure up and order them for you so the quantities and the layout work together.

How do I get a price?

Send through the room sizes, what you want tiled and a photo or two. For bathrooms and wet areas a site measure keeps the quote accurate, and the quote is free.
