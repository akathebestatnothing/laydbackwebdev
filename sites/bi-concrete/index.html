What we do

Three disciplines, one crew. Every stage is set out and braced before a metre of concrete goes in.

  1. 01

    Formwork

    Footpaths, suspended slabs, walls and columns. Shuttering built and set to spec, stripped clean for the next pour.

  2. 02

    Steelfixing

    Reinforcement scheduled, placed and tied. Mesh, bar and cage fabrication for footings, slabs and structural elements.

  3. 03

    Concrete construction

    Place, screed, finish, cure. Footpaths through to structural work, with handover records supplied on completion.

Brady runs every job. No layers between the quote and the pour.

B.I Concrete Constructions Pty Ltd works out of Norah Head across the Central Coast, Newcastle and Lake Macquarie. Recent work has run through Toronto, Boolaroo, Kulnura and Medowie.

Formwork, steel and concrete are handled by the same crew, so the set-out, the pour and the finish stay accountable to one person.
