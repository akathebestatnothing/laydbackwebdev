What we do

Specialist concrete services

Four focused capabilities — no generalist filler. Each job scoped, executed, and left clean.

01

Diamond core drilling

Precise holes for services, anchors, penetrations — wet and dry, handheld to rig-mounted, up to 600mm diameter.

02

Wall chasing

Clean channels for electrical, plumbing, data — twin-blade chase saws, depth controlled, minimal vibration.

03

Concrete cutting & sawing

Flat sawing, wall sawing, wire sawing — door/window openings, expansion joints, slab cutting to depth.

04

Controlled demolition

Selective removal, robotic demolition, bursting and splitting — tight access, vibration-sensitive sites.

Equipment & capability

Built for the job

Right-sized fleet, maintained to standard, operated by the people who quoted the work.

Rig-mounted core drills

Husqvarna, Tyrolit, Hilti — 152–600mm, wet & dry, dust extraction fitted.

Twin-blade chase saws

Husqvarna K4000 / K6500 — adjustable width 15–50mm, depth to 150mm.

Walk-behind flat saws

Wire sawing

Robotic demolition

Brokk 110 / 170 — electric, remote operated, tight access, low vibration.

Bursting & splitting
