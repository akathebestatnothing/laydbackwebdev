02

Mix → Hopper → Wall.

Every job follows the same sequence — no shortcuts, no cold joints, one continuous application.

01

Mix

Sand, cement, and additives measured by weight — not shovel — into the forced-action mixer. Consistency locked before the first drop.

02

Load

Mix discharges into the hopper. Pressure and flow calibrated. No air pockets, no segregation — just a steady column of material.

03

Base Coat Pass

Spray/trowel lays the first skin. Thickness gauged, straight-edge ruled. Grain open for mechanical key — this is the skeleton.

04

Finish Coat Pass

Second sweep follows immediately — same hopper, adjusted mix. Grain tightens, colour locks, straight edge cuts the clean line. Done.

03

Asked on every site.

Do you colour-match existing render?
Yes — we tint the acrylic or texture coat to match the surrounding finish. A test patch is standard before the full pass.
What substrates do you work over?
Brick, block, concrete, FC sheet, blueboard, and existing render (after soundness check and bond coat).
How long before the finish coat goes on?
Wet-on-wet where the system allows; otherwise 24-48 hours for cement base to firm up. The schedule is set in the quote.
Is the work guaranteed?
Workmanship warranty applies — details in the written quote. We use specified mix ratios and branded acrylics only.
What areas do you cover?
Greater metro and surrounds — call or use the quote form with your postcode and we'll confirm.
04

Why the wall holds.

Forced-Action Mixer

Every batch mixed by weight in a planetary mixer — no batch variance, no dry pockets, consistent water-cement ratio.

Hopper-Fed Application

Material flows from hopper to spray gun at regulated pressure. No hand-loading, no air entrapment, even coverage.

Two-Coat System

Base coat keys mechanically. Finish coat seals and colours. Grain shift is visible — coarse to fine — not a marketing claim.

Straight Edge Rule

Every pass ruled with a straight edge. No bellies, no hollows, no waves. The line is the spec.

Branded Acrylics Only

Dulux Acratex, Rockcote, Unitex — specified by system, not by price. Data sheets on request.

Written Quote, Fixed Scope

Mix ratios, coat thicknesses, texture spec, and warranty terms in writing before we start. No verbal variations.
