Privacy policy

  • Technical data such as IP address, browser type, pages visited and referral source.

How we use it

  • To respond to your enquiry and provide a quote.
  • To schedule and carry out the work you authorise.
  • To improve the website and services, using aggregate data.

Sharing

We do not sell your data. We may share it with our booking and invoicing software to fulfil your job, or with authorities where the law requires it.

Your rights

You may ask for access, correction or deletion of your personal information at any time by calling us or using the quote form.

Quote data is kept for 24 months after the last contact, then deleted unless legal compliance requires otherwise.
