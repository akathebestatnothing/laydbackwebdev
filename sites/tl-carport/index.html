What we build

Outdoor structures for Melbourne homes

Every job is designed and built by our own team, from footings to flashings. Steel or timber frames, Colorbond roofing.

01

Verandah design & construction

Gable, skillion, flat or curved roofs, set out to the line of your house and the way you actually use the space.

02

Carport design & construction

Single, double and multi-bay carports, with storage worked into the frame where the site allows.

03

Pergolas, patios & shade

Open pergolas, paved patios and shade structures, detailed to suit the block rather than a catalogue.

Built to an engineer's specification. Council permits arranged where they apply.

How we work

From footings to finished

A fixed scope agreed in writing before we start, then four stages in order, with nothing skipped.

01

Design & permits

Site measure, drawings, engineering and council paperwork. You approve the plans first.

02

Footings & frames

Footings poured to specification, then posts, beams and rafters stood and braced.

03

Roof & flashings

Sheets fixed bay by bay, ridge capping and flashings sealed so the structure is watertight.

04

Handover

Walkthrough, paperwork and warranty documents, then the job is yours.
