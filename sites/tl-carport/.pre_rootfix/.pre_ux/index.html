What We Build

Outdoor Structures for Melbourne Homes

Every project is designed and constructed by our team - no subcontractors, no shortcuts.

Verandah Design & Construction

Custom verandahs that extend your living space outdoors. Gable, skillion, flat or curved roofs - designed for your home's architecture and Melbourne's weather.

Carport Design & Construction

Single, double, and multi-bay carports with integrated storage options. Steel or timber frames, Colorbond roofing - built to protect your vehicles year-round.

Custom Outdoor Structures

Pergolas, patios, outdoor kitchens, and shade structures. If you can dream it, we build it - tailored to your site, budget, and lifestyle.

How We Work

From Footings to Finished

A clear, fixed-price process - no surprises, no hidden variations.

Design & Permits

Site measure, 3D design, engineering, and council permits handled end-to-end. You approve the plans before we break ground.

Footings & Frames

Concrete footings poured to engineer's spec. Steel or timber posts, beams, and rafters erected - the frame goes up fast.

Roof & Flashings

Colorbond sheets fixed bay by bay. Ridge capping, flashings, and gutters sealed watertight. The structure is weatherproof.

Handover

Final walk-through, paperwork, and warranty docs. Your keys, your car rolls in - job complete.
