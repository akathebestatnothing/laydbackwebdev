What we do

Concrete for every part of the home

From the drive you arrive on to the patio you entertain on - every slab gets the same prep, reinforcement and finish discipline.

01

Driveways

Formed and reinforced driveways - plain, coloured or exposed aggregate - built to take the weight and the weather.

02

House Slabs

Waffle pod and conventional raft slabs to engineer's spec - vapour barrier, reinforcement, Termimesh and inspection ready.

03

Paths & Walkways

Front entries, side access and garden paths - formed to fall, broom or exposed finish, cut joints where needed.

04

Patios & Alfresco

Entertainment slabs at the right level - flush to internal floors, soaked for pavers or left as a finished surface.

05

Exposed Aggregate

Seeded and washed finishes in a range of stone - non-slip, hard-wearing and the classic Australian driveway look.

06

Decorative Finishes

Coloured oxides, stencil patterns, saw-cut geometry and trowel textures - detail work that lifts a plain slab.

Finishes

The surface you see and feel

Finish isn't cosmetic - it's grip, drainage, wear resistance and how the slab ages. We match the finish to the job.

Choose your finish

Broom Finish
Standard non-slip texture - consistent strokes, even fall, clean edges.
Exposed Aggregate
Seeded stone washed to reveal texture - tough, timeless, high traction.
Saw-Cut Pattern
Control joints cut to a grid or geometric layout - planned cracking, clean lines.
Coloured Oxide
Integral colour through the mix - no surface coating to peel or wear through.
Stencil / Stamp
Patterned texture - pavers, tile, slate or custom - sealed for longevity.
Steel Trowel
Smooth, dense internal slabs - ready for tiles, vinyl or polished overlay.
