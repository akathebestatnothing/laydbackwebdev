What we do

Four trades, one standard

Paving

Driveways, paths, courtyards and pool surrounds in stone, brick and concrete pavers. Laid on prepared base with precision joints.

Landscaping

Site preparation, drainage, planting, turf, edging and garden construction. Soft and hard elements integrated from ground up.

Concreting

Slabs, footpaths, crossovers, footings and decorative finishes. Formed, poured and finished to Australian Standards.

Retaining Walls

Block, sleeper and stone walls engineered for cut/fill sites. Drainage included. Council compliance where required.

Who we are

Groundwork first, finish clean

SDC Landscaping and Maintenance is a construction landscaping business run by Samuel Cooper from Cameron Park, servicing Newcastle, Hunter and Lake Macquarie.

We build outdoor spaces from the substrate up — excavation, base preparation, drainage, then the visible finish. Every job ships with inspection records and handover documentation.

Services: paving, landscaping, concreting, retaining walls.
