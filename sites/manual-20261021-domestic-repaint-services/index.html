What we do

Garage floors, epoxy coatings and repainting for homes across the Sunshine Coast

Garage Floor Repaints

Grind, repair and recoat tired concrete garage floors with a hard-wearing finish that hoses down clean.

Epoxy Floor Coatings

Solid epoxy and topcoat systems for garages, patios, sheds and utility areas, laid to a sealed and primed slab.

Flake & Quartz Finishes

Decorative flake or quartz broadcast into the base coat for a textured, non-slip floor with a cleanable topcoat.

Interior Repainting

Walls, ceilings, trims and doors cut in and rolled out, with furniture and floors sheeted before we start.

Exterior Repainting

Render, weatherboard, fascia, eaves and posts — washed, patched and coated for coastal sun and salt air.

Prep & Repairs

Pressure cleaning, sanding, filling, gap sealing and mould or efflorescence treatment before any paint goes on.

Domestic Repaint Services

We are a Sunshine Coast painting crew working on homes — garage floors and epoxy coatings on one side, interior and exterior repainting on the other. Same preparation, same care, same clean-up whichever job it is.

Concrete gets ground, filled and primed before a coating ever goes near it, because an epoxy floor is only as good as the slab under it. Repaints get sheeted, patched and sanded the same way. You get a written scope, a start date and one point of contact through the job.

  • Residential work only — no site crews, no subcontract chains
  • Floor coatings specified for the slab and the traffic
  • Drop sheets, masking and a clean handover every day
  • Local to the Sunshine Coast, from Caloundra to Noosa
