The workshop

AT Engineering is a hands-on fabrication team working out of 95 Young Road, Whitton. We work closely with clients to land on a solution that meets the real requirements of the job, then we build it, deliver it and stand behind it.

The work runs from stainless steel tanks and pressure vessels through to structural steel, conveying systems, agricultural equipment and CNC cutting. We fabricate for mining, agriculture, manufacturing, meat processing and food production, across Eastern Australia.

  • End-to-end fabrication, from design through to delivery
  • Stainless steel, structural steel and CNC metal processing
  • Built for industrial and agricultural duty
  • Workshop in Whitton NSW, servicing Eastern Australia
