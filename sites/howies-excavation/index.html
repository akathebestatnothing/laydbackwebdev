Howie's Excavation & Construction is a Rathmines-based earthworks outfit working across Lake Macquarie and the wider Hunter. We handle the ground side of residential and civil projects — the cuts, the levels, the drainage and the retaining that everything else gets built on.

One crew, one point of contact. You deal with the person running the machine, so the job gets done to the level you actually asked for.
