What we do

Scoped on site, priced per job or by day rate. No guesswork, no hidden extras.

  1. 01

    Dig-outs & site preparation

    Excavation, clearing, benching and pad prep for sheds, yards, driveways and hardstands. Machine matched to the ground conditions on the day.

  2. 02

    Spreading & grading

    Gravel, road base and crusher dust spread, watered and rolled to spec. Laser level finish available on tracks and pads.

  3. 03

    Concrete prep assistance

    Formwork support, reinforcement placement and a screed-ready subgrade. The base gets right so the pour goes clean.

  4. 04

    Rural labour

    Fencing, dam cleaning, weed spraying, livestock handling and general site labour. Skilled hands, day rate or project scoped.
