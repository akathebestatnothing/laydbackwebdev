What we do

Timber Flooring

Solid and engineered timber laid on the right underlay, with expansion and doorways detailed properly.

Hybrid Flooring

Water-resistant hybrid planks for kitchens, laundries and busy hallways, laid tight and level.

Vinyl Plank

Hard-wearing plank vinyl for rentals, offices and wet areas, over a properly levelled subfloor.

Laminate Flooring

Subfloor Preparation

Levelling, grinding and patching so the boards go down flat, without hollow spots or squeaks.

Sanding & Polishing

Existing timber floors stripped back, sanded and polished to bring the grain and colour back.

About Tiger Flooring

Tiger Flooring supplies and lays timber, hybrid, vinyl plank and laminate flooring, and sands and polishes existing timber floors.

What is under the boards decides how the floor ages, so subfloor preparation gets done first: levelling, patching, and a flat surface to lay onto.

  • Timber, hybrid, vinyl plank and laminate
  • Subfloor levelling and preparation
  • Sanding and polishing of existing floors
