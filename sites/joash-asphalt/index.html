How a patch is done

Cut, fill, compact, seal

Every patch follows the same discipline — clean edges, hot mix at temperature, proper compaction, seamless finish.

01

Cut & prep

Failed square marked and cut with a road saw — clean, vertical edges. Loose material excavated, base inspected and re-compacted.

02

Tack coat

Bitumen emulsion brushed on vertical faces and base — bonds new hot mix to existing surfaces, stops water tracking.

03

Hot mix placed

Asphalt barrowed in hot, raked level slightly proud. Joints staggered, hand-worked into corners, zero segregation.

04

Roll & finish

Plate compactor passes until flush and dense. Steam drifts off the fresh mat. Edges sealed, traffic-ready in minutes.
