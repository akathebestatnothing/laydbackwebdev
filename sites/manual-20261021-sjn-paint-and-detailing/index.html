What we do

Paint and detailing work for cars, utes and vans, done in the bay.

Machine Paint Correction

Swirls, holograms and light scratches cut back by machine and refined down to a clear, even finish.

Ceramic Coating

A ceramic layer applied over corrected paint to hold the gloss and make washing easier.

Cut & Polish

Paint decontaminated, machine cut and polished for depth and shine.

Spray Painting

Panels and parts sprayed to match, from a single guard to a full respray.

Interior Detailing

Interior cleaned right through: carpets, seats, trim and glass, without soaking the car.

Paint Decontamination

Tar, iron and bonded fallout removed with clay and chemical decontamination before polishing.

About SJN Paint & Detailing

SJN Paint & Detailing is a paint and detailing shop in Bathurst, working on cars, utes and vans.

Paint correction starts with preparation: the car is washed and decontaminated, the paint is measured, then the machine work is stepped down until the finish is even under a light.

  • Machine paint correction and cut and polish
  • Ceramic coating over corrected paint
  • Spray painting and panel work
