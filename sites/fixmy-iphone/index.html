What comes across the bench

Six kinds of job, one bench standard: test the fault, quote it, then do the work clean.

Screen replacement

Phones and tablets

Cracked glass, dead pixels, touch that has stopped tracking. Panel swapped on the jig, frame reseated, touch and digitiser tested before it leaves the bench.

  • 02

    Battery swap

    Rapid drain, swelling, shutdowns under load. New cell fitted, adhesive renewed, charged through and checked.

  • 03

    Charge port repair

    Loose fit, no charge, pocket lint packed into the port. Cleaned, or the port replaced when cleaning is not enough.

  • 04

    Water damage diagnostics

    Corrosion scan under the lamp, ultrasonic clean, board-level repair where the damage leaves something to work with.

  • 05

    Tablet and iPad repair

    Screen, battery and port work on larger devices, on the same jig and with the same test pass at the end.

  • 06

    Data recovery assist

    Board-level access for contacts, photos and notes when a device will not boot but the memory is still good.
