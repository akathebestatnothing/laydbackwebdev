What we build, and how it gets built

Every job runs off a set out and a level. Forms go up square, steel goes in tied, and the pour is screeded flat to a rail.

  1. 01

    Slabs and footings

    Formwork set out to line and level, reinforcement placed and tied, then poured and screeded to a flat surface.

  2. 02

    Driveways and paved areas

    Formed with falls set so water runs away from the building instead of sitting against it.

  3. 03

    Formwork

    Panels, bracing, tie rods and spacer blocks, checked for line before a metre of concrete goes in.

  4. 04

    Screeding and finishing

    Screeded across a straight rail to the wet edge, then edged and floated, with joints cut on line.

  5. 05

    Strip and make good

    Forms struck once the concrete has gone off, edges cleaned up and the site left tidy.

The line, the level, then the pour

Nothing gets poured until the set out is checked and the forms are braced both ways.

  1. A

    Set out

    Worked off the drawings, with the levels checked before the first panel stands.

  2. B

    Form and tie

    Panels set true to line, braced both ways and tied with rods, wire and spacer blocks.

  3. C

    Pour and screed

    Steel tied, concrete placed, then screeded across the rail to the wet edge.
